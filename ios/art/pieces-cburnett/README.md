# Pieces: Classic

(https://github.com/lichess-org/lila/tree/master/public/piece/cburnett), licensed under the GNU GPL, version 2
or later (https://www.gnu.org/licenses/gpl-2.0.txt), as recorded in Lichess's COPYING.md
(https://github.com/lichess-org/lila/blob/master/COPYING.md):

    public/piece/cburnett | [Colin M.L. Burnett](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/User:Cburnett) | [GPLv2+](https://www.gnu.org/licenses/gpl-2.0.txt)

Used here under GPL-3.0, which "or later" allows and which combines with this app's AGPL-3.0.

The files here are unmodified. `ios/scripts/pieces.py` recolors them in Zeist's ivory and ebony with a soft
light and renders the images the app uses.
