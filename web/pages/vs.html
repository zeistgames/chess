Chess

  1. Checkmate wins: the king is attacked and has no way out.
  2. White moves first. Castling, en passant and promotion follow the usual rules.
  3. A draw comes by itself: no legal move while not in check, the same position three times, 50 moves each with no capture or pawn move, or too few pieces to checkmate.
  4. With a clock, running out of time loses. If the other side can't checkmate, it's a draw.
  5. Resigning loses the game.
  6. Games against {{NAME}} aren't rated. They're kept in this browser.

Tap a piece, then where it goes, or drag it. On a keyboard, the left and right arrows step through the moves.
